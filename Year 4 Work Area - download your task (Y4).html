How to start your work
  1. Open Documents and make a folder with your class and name, like Y3-Ali.
  2. Press the orange Download button.
  3. In the Save box, click Documents, then your folder, then press Save.
  4. Open the file from your folder and do the task.
  5. Press Ctrl + S now and then to save your work.
